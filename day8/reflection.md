The most difficult concept for me was concurrency: two people can read the same seat as available and try to buy it at nearly the same time. I understood the idea more clearly by following one seat through its states: available, held, and sold. Thinking through overlapping requests showed me why a page's availability display cannot guarantee a successful purchase. A database transaction, row lock, hold expiry, and unique constraint provide the actual protection against selling one seat twice.
