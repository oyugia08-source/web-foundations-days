The hardest concept for me in this capstone was concurrency: two buyers can see the same seat as available and attempt to claim it at once. I worked through it by tracing both requests through the seat states, from available to held to sold. That made clear why a seat-map display cannot reserve inventory; the transaction, row lock, expiry check, and unique ticket constraint must decide the winner.
